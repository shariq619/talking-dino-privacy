Talking Dino Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Operator: Pulse Labs HQ (Pulse Labs)

1. Introduction

Talking Dino is a virtual pet and casual game that allows users to interact with a dinosaur, play mini-games, customize their experience, create illustrated images, and record short voice clips for playback.

This Privacy Policy explains how Talking Dino handles information, including information processed locally on your device and information that may be processed by third-party services.

Talking Dino is designed for a young audience, including children and teenagers. Protecting children's privacy is an important part of our application design.

2. Accounts and Local Game Data

Talking Dino does not require users to create an account.

The app stores information such as game progress, scores, virtual coins, dinosaur customizations, achievements, preferences and settings locally on the device.

The app does not currently provide an account-operated cloud synchronization service for this game data.

Some eligible app-private data may be included in Android's system backup, depending on device settings and Android backup policies.

3. Microphone and Voice Recordings

Talking Dino requests microphone permission when users choose to use its voice-recording features.

Recordings are used for in-app playback and voice effects. The gameplay implementation does not intentionally upload these recordings to a Talking Dino-operated server.

When a user chooses the Share Voice feature, the app may create a temporary copy and open Android's system sharing interface.

The selected receiving app or service may process information according to its own privacy policy. Users and their parents or guardians should exercise care when sharing voice recordings.

Microphone permission can be managed through Android device settings.

4. Photo Studio and Images

Photo Studio generates illustrated images using content available within the application.

It does not require taking a camera photograph or importing existing photographs.

Users may choose to save generated images to their device gallery or share them using Android's sharing interface.

Images saved to the gallery may remain on the device after Talking Dino is uninstalled or its game progress is reset.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Talking Dino includes Google Mobile Ads SDK technology in development builds. Advertising features may be enabled in future published versions after the required testing and privacy compliance reviews.

Where advertising is enabled, the app is intended to request age-appropriate, non-personalized advertisements using child-directed privacy settings.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share information including:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App interactions and advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Device or other identifiers, subject to the application's configuration and applicable privacy restrictions.

This information may be processed by Google for purposes including ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related services.

Talking Dino does not intend to use interest-based advertising or remarketing for children.

Advertising availability and related data processing depend on the version of the application, applicable regional requirements and the advertising configuration.

For information about Google's data practices, visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

Talking Dino's Google Play target audience includes children aged 5 and under, 6–8, 9–12, 13–15 and 16–17.

We aim to limit unnecessary collection of children's information and use age-appropriate advertising controls where advertisements are enabled.

We do not intend to request personalized advertising or remarketing for children.

Children should involve a parent or guardian before sharing voice recordings, images or other personal information with external services.

Where additional parental controls or restrictions are required, those safeguards must be implemented and verified before the affected functionality is offered.

7. Third-Party Services

Talking Dino uses Android, Expo and React Native technologies and may include Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform services in applicable builds.

These services may process technical information according to their functionality, configuration and privacy policies.

The presence and activity of third-party services may differ between development and publicly released versions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game information generally remains on the device until it is reset, removed through application storage controls, or deleted as part of uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Temporary voice files may be removed during application cleanup or when Android clears cached storage.

Files saved to the user's gallery or shared with another application are not necessarily deleted when Talking Dino's local data is removed.

For privacy questions or requests relating to the application, contact the operator using the email address below.

9. Security

We aim to use appropriate technical controls to protect the application's information.

No electronic storage or data transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Third-party service data handling and encryption practices are also governed by those services' implementations and policies.

10. Privacy Choices

Users may manage microphone permissions through Android settings.

Where required and supported, additional advertising privacy choices may be made available through the application's Data & Privacy screen.

The availability of these controls depends on applicable requirements and the published application version.

Parents and guardians may contact the operator with questions about children's privacy.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Talking Dino's features, advertising configuration, data practices or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
