What Talking Dino does

Talking Dino is a virtual pet game. You can care for Dino, play mini-games, customize its appearance and room, make illustrated images, and record a short voice clip for local playback. You can use the app without creating an account.

Microphone and voice clips

Talking Dino asks for microphone permission when you start recording. A clip can last up to 15 seconds. The app uses the recording for local playback, including an optional faster “Funny Voice” playback mode. The voice recording is kept in app cache while the Talking screen is active and is removed when you leave that screen or replace the recording, subject to device cleanup timing. The gameplay code does not upload voice clips to an app-operated server. You may deny microphone permission and use other activities.

If you choose Share Voice, the app makes a separate temporary copy and opens your device's share sheet. The app removes copies older than 24 hours on a later launch or share; a copy may remain longer if the app is not opened. The operating system may remove cache files sooner. The app does not control how an app or person you select stores, uses or forwards the clip. Funny Voice playback effects are not applied to the original file you share.

Images and gallery

Photo Studio creates an illustrated image from Dino, backgrounds, frames and stickers inside the app. It does not take a camera photo or import your existing photos. If you choose Save Photo, the app requests photo-library permission needed by the device and writes the generated image to your gallery. If you choose Share Photo or another Share action, the app opens the share sheet with an image card. Gallery files and copies received by other apps are not removed when you reset Talking Dino.

Local game data

The app stores your chosen Dino name, game scores, coins, care status, rewards, achievements, customizations and settings on your device so your game can continue later. The current game has no account or cloud synchronization for this data. Settings → Reset Progress replaces the saved player data with default values on that device. It does not delete files already saved to the gallery or sent through another app.

Internet and third-party software

The current gameplay code has no app-operated account, backend for voice or progress, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or in-app purchase integration. It uses Expo and React Native software modules for audio, files, images, sharing and device storage. The Android app declares internet access for its framework and included libraries. A chosen share destination, Google Play, the operating system and other device services may process information under their own policies. Opening this policy website from Settings sends a web request to its GitHub Pages host, which may handle ordinary web request data under its own practices. The operator must complete a final review of embedded SDK network behavior before publishing this policy or making a “no data collected” Play declaration.

Children and families

The intended audience includes children aged 5 and under, 6–8 and 9–12, as well as adults. Voice recording and sharing can contain a child's personal information. The current build offers a preview and asks the user to confirm before opening the system share sheet, but does not yet have an adult control for sharing. Adults should supervise recording and sharing. The operator must resolve this safeguard and review applicable children's privacy requirements before releasing to a child-targeted track.

Retention and requests

Local progress remains on the device until reset, app removal, or device-level deletion. Original voice clips and temporary share copies follow the cache behavior described above. We cannot delete files you saved to your gallery or that another app or recipient retained. For a privacy request or question, contact muhammadshariqali@gmail.com. Because the app has no user account or app-operated cloud save in the audited version, a device's Reset Progress control is the available in-app way to remove local game progress; the operator cannot remotely erase that device's files.

Changes

If app behavior or this policy changes, Pulse Labs will update this page and its effective date. Contact muhammadshariqali@gmail.com with questions.
