About Talking Dino

Talking Dino is a virtual pet game. You can care for Dino, play mini-games, change its appearance and room, create Dino artwork, and record a short voice clip for local playback. You do not need an account to use the app.

Children and advertising

Talking Dino's Google Play target audience covers ages 0–17 (5 and under, 6–8, 9–12, 13–15, and 16–17). The prepared release asks for one broad age group before play and before app-authored advertising initialization. It does not collect a birthday, and users may choose "Prefer not to say." The choice is stored only in app-private storage. In Android versionCode 8, every choice, including unknown and older, receives child-directed and under-age treatment, a maximum G content rating, and non-personalized ad requests. Production advertising is enabled in Android versionCode 8. A labeled, non-personalized Google AdMob banner may appear only in the Data & Privacy footer, never over gameplay.

Those settings govern ad requests; they do not mean that Google receives no data. Parents and guardians may contact us about the app's data practices. Where local law requires consent or parental authorization, advertising must not start until those requirements are met.

Game data and Android backup

The app stores Dino's name, coins, care state, achievements, unlocked items, scores, customizations, and preferences in app-private storage. We do not operate an account or our own cloud-sync service for this data. Android's backup service may include eligible app data in a user's backup account or a device-to-device transfer, depending on device settings and the installed version.

Settings > Reset Progress replaces local game progress and preferences with defaults, but does not clear the separate broad age-group choice. Settings > Privacy > Change age group clears that choice and asks again. Removing the app or clearing its storage normally removes the choice and private game data; an Android backup may restore eligible app data. Reset Progress does not remove photos saved to the gallery, copies held by other apps, or data already retained in a device backup.

Microphone and voice clips

The app asks for microphone access only when a user starts recording. A clip lasts at most 15 seconds and is used for local Dino playback. The Talking screen clears its cached clip when it is replaced or the user leaves the screen, subject to device cleanup timing. Other activities work without microphone permission. In-app voice sharing needs a grown-up: every share first asks a quick maths question that only an adult is expected to answer. After the grown-up answers correctly, choosing Share Voice opens Android's share sheet and transfers a temporary copy to the app the user selects. Older versions either hid Share Voice or offered it without an adult control.

Photo Studio and gallery

Photo Studio creates Dino artwork inside the app; it does not import camera photos. A user can choose Save Photo to copy a generated image to the device gallery. The app does not upload the image to our servers. In-app image sharing needs a grown-up: every share first asks a quick maths question that only an adult is expected to answer. After the grown-up answers correctly, choosing Share Photo opens Android's share sheet. Once saved or shared, the device gallery, selected recipient and other apps may handle that copy under their own settings and policies. Reset Progress does not delete gallery images.

Google AdMob data

The prepared Android release source includes Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); the reviewed development APK packages Mobile Ads SDK version 25.5.0 and UMP version 4.0.0. In Android versionCode 8, the app requests a banner only after the broad age-group choice and under-age UMP check permit it. A development privacy check may contact Google for consent-message information without requesting an ad. An adult developer can separately opt in to an official Google demo banner on a controlled development device; production requests use the owner's AdMob banner unit. A labeled production AdMob banner may appear only in the Data & Privacy footer, separate from game controls. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK version 25.5.0 automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic data, such as app or SDK launch time, hang rate, and energy use; and
  • device or account identifiers, which may include Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers related to signed-in device accounts.

The prepared Android configuration blocks the advertising-ID permission; the reviewed development APK does not request it. This restriction does not rule out other identifiers or the data types above. We do not add Dino names, game progress, voice recordings, or generated artwork to ad requests. Google says data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's data retention and controls are described in its linked policies.

Read Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for Google's practices and controls.

Other services and links

Talking Dino uses Expo and React Native software for audio, files, images, local storage, and navigation. Opening the full privacy policy from Settings opens a website in the device browser. That website's host and the browser may receive ordinary web request information under their own policies. When a share-sheet action is used, the selected recipient app handles the shared file under its own policy.

Retention and privacy requests

Local game data remains until reset, app removal, or device-level deletion. Cached voice clips follow the Talking screen behavior above. Gallery images remain until deleted from the gallery. We cannot remove copies controlled by the device backup service, another app, or a recipient. Reset Progress does not delete information processed by Google Mobile Ads.

For privacy questions or requests, contact muhammadshariqali@gmail.com. We will review requests under applicable law and direct questions about Google-held advertising information to the relevant Google controls or request channels where appropriate. We cannot remotely erase files stored only on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The last-updated date will change when a revised policy is published.
